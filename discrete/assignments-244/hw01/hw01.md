
## Question 1

> Use a direct proof to show that the sum of two even integers is even

Let $a$ and $b$ be two even integers. Then, by definition, there exist integers $m$ and $n$ such that $a = 2m$ and $b = 2n$. 

Then, the sum of $a$ and $b$ is:

$2m + 2n = 2(m + n)$

Since integers are closed under addition, $m + n$ is an integer. Therefore, $2(m + n)$ is even.

Thus, the sum of two even integers is even. $\blacksquare$


## Question 2

> Show that the additive inverse, or negative, of an even number is an even number using a direct
proof.


Let $a$ be an even integer. Then, by definition, there exists an integer $m$ such that $a = 2m$.

The additive inverse of $a$ is $-a$. 

Then, $-a = -2m = 2(-m)$

Since integers are closed under multiplication, $-1 \cdot m$ is an integer and $2(-m)$ is therefore even.

Therefore, the additive inverse of an even number is an even number. $\blacksquare$

## Question 3

> Use a direct proof to show that the product of two odd numbers is odd.

Let $a$ and $b$ be two odd integers. Then, by definition, there exist integers $m$ and $n$ such that $a = 2m + 1$ and $b = 2n + 1$. 

Then, the product of $a$ and $b$ is:

$(2m + 1)(2n + 1) = 4mn + 2m + 2n + 1 = 2(2mn + m + n) + 1$

Since integers are closed under addition and multiplication, $2mn + m + n$ is an integer and $2(2mn + m + n)$ is therefore even. Thus, $2(2mn + m + n) + 1$ is odd.

Therefore, the product of two odd numbers is odd. $\blacksquare$
