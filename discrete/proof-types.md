
- **contraposition**: assume !q
  - and show that !p is implied
- **contradiction**: !q but then just assume p
  - and prove that this leads to a contradiction
- **exhaustion**
- **counterexample** (for disproving a statement)
- **non-constructive**: proof by contradiction of an existence of something, showing that !E(x) is impossible
- **constructive**: witness of existence of something
- **uniqueness**: showing that E(x) and E(y) are both true and x != y
